About Me

Careful work matters.

I’m Michael, a Data Analyst with a background in Economics & Statistics from University of Benin.

I didn’t start out calling myself a Data Analyst. For a long time I was always called upon by colleagues when their Excel sheet wasn’t adding up, when reports had different numbers in different places.

I learned to like that part. The inspection. The cleaning. The part where you sit with a messy file and make it make sense.

During my service at the Ministry of Trade and Industries, that’s mainly what I did. I managed large numbers of personnel datasets. I built monthly reports that management actually waited for. I created validation checks because accuracy wasn’t optional, it was the whole job. And when the manual cleaning became too much, I started writing Python scripts to do it faster and cleaner.

I’m comfortable in Advanced Excel and Python for data clean up automation.

I’m open to opportunities where this kind of careful work matters.

Featured • 1,000+ Rows

HR Data Cleaning & Preprocessing Pipeline

A synthetic but realistic HR dataset with 1,000+ rows and 13 columns, deliberately messy to reflect the files you actually receive at work.

What was messy: Combined fields, inconsistent date formats, salary values mixed with “N/A”, missing ages, inconsistent text values, and duplicate records.

What I did: Inspected first (shape, nulls, duplicates, value counts), then built a reusable cleaning pipeline — standardized column names, split Department_Region into two usable columns, fixed dates, converted salary and age to numeric, standardized text fields, removed duplicates, and logged every change.

Result: A clean, analysis-ready dataset with correct data types and two new useful columns. More importantly, a reusable script that works on next month’s file — not just this one.

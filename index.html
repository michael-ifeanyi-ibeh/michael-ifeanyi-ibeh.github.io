About Me

I'm Michael Ifeanyi Ibeh, a Cloud DevOps Engineer passionate about making deployments boring — in a good way. I believe infrastructure should be automated, observable, and self-healing.

My journey started with Linux and networking (Cisco background) and evolved into Cloud and IaC. I spend most of my time building with AWS, Terraform, Docker, Nginx, and Bash — turning manual, error-prone steps into one-command, repeatable workflows.

What I bring to a team:

  • Zero-downtime mindset: Blue-Green deployments, health checks, reverse proxy failover
  • IaC first: Everything in Terraform, versioned, with remote state
  • Automation over documentation: If it’s manual twice, I script it

Currently exploring advanced Kubernetes (EKS) and GitOps with ArgoCD. Open to remote DevOps roles and freelance infra automation.
